SYNTHETIC PUBLIC AGREEMENT — CalyxQuay Beacon Cache
Version CQ-2031-04. Fictional test-engineering material; no real vendor or customer.

1. Covered service and monthly period
CalyxQuay supplies Beacon Cache on the Lantern plan. This agreement covers that
service for the single monthly period from 2031-04-01T00:00:00Z inclusive to
2031-05-01T00:00:00Z exclusive. The monthly period is exactly 30 days, or
43,200 minutes. All times are UTC. There are no other covered services or periods.

2. Availability and credit
Monthly availability is 100 times (43,200 minus unavailable minutes) divided by
43,200. If monthly availability is below 99.9%, the customer receives a service
credit of 12% of eligible monthly service fees. Availability equal to or above
99.9% earns no credit. There is one threshold and one credit rate.

3. Fee base and exclusion
Eligible monthly service fees are the Beacon Cache recurring subscription line
for the covered monthly period on the customer's paid invoice, in USD. Exclude
operator coaching fees from the credit base. Round the credit to the nearest
cent, half up. A service credit is not a cash refund.

4. Filing deadline
The timestamped claim must be filed within 14 days after the monthly period
ends, no later than 2031-05-15T00:00:00Z. Filing exactly at that timestamp is
permitted. A claim filed later receives no credit.

5. Required evidence
A claim must include a verifiable itemized paid invoice identifying the account,
service, plan, monthly period, subscription fee and any operator coaching fee.
It must also include a verifiable CalyxQuay status record identifying the account,
Beacon Cache service, Lantern plan, this monthly period, and unavailable minutes.
The status record is authoritative for availability. Invoice and status records
must have source identifiers, recorded timestamps and content hashes. Without
either required record, entitlement is unknown until that record is supplied.
The claim's filing timestamp is required. Customer telemetry is not required.

6. Denials and authority
A denial correctly citing a late filing or availability at or above the threshold
must be accepted. If a denial requests a missing required record, supply that
record when available and reassess. If the denial introduces an unexplained
exception not present here, escalate for review; do not invent an exception or
send an automatic appeal. This agreement authorizes synthetic testing only.
