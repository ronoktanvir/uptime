# CalyxQuay local permission review

The team authorized preparation of a limited exception, not its activation. This record will contain the exact unsigned proposal after the implementation is integrated and tested. Until operator approval and reviewer signing are completed, Ronok's existing session remains SHADOW with consequential actions disabled.

The proposed permission allows Ronok to use the ordinary interpretation, approval, activation and rollback controls for one exact CalyxQuay adapter in one separate local workspace, for at most 48 hours. It grants no claims, disputes, payment, credit, policy, model or Ramp authority. It does not represent a customer's contract review or independent release certification.

Two steps are required after reviewing the concrete proposal: the local operator approves its exact hash into the fixed protected host authority store; Ronok signs the same proposal with his enrolled key. The launcher must be explicitly pointed at the signed envelope. Merely possessing the unsigned proposal, reviewer key or a signature does not enable the exception.

The host authority retains revocation and terminal rollback status outside the workspace. Expired, revoked, changed-source or changed-version authority cannot be reused; existing history is preserved. A new grant requires a new operator approval. The host administrator remains trusted, so this is not a claim of protection against an administrator replacing the entire authority store.

The exact commands, tested commit, proposal hash, version and expiry will be added after verification. Do not execute guessed commands or modify the prepared JSON. [Decision and Opus criticism](decisions/ADR-0088-local-review-exception.md).
