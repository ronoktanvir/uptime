## What now works

The public/private structural join uses the accepted public agreement and product identifiers alongside the private agreement/account/resource binding. The public bytes retain their exact extraction digest. The runner only consumes `UPTIME_PUBLIC_EXTRACTIONS` with `UPTIME_MODEL_PROPOSITIONS=1`; it invokes no model, reads no secret credentials and permits no external network connection.

Promotion follows normal candidate, shadow-test, interpretation, approval, activation and case-dispatch routes. The independent half-percent-loss control derives its expected arithmetic from the public percentage table, independently of the compiler's tier values. It distinguishes the ordinary availability formula from the competing grouping. Case GET readback checks ID, outcome, amount and version. Rollback rejects new dispatch while preserving historical readback. Every approval is explicitly SCRIPTED in a disposable workspace.

Inbound evidence uses separate ephemeral Ed25519 sources, exact grants and full-scope binding. Opaque source locators that do not fit the wire ID grammar become SHA-256 references with retained input-to-delivery provenance. Resource allocation joins require matching account, resource, invoice/incident identity and interval. Discount amounts must reconcile to the net recurring line. A complete billing statement additionally needs exact monthly bounds, a reconciled invoice total and a unique scoped recurring line. An aggregate prior-credit amount is retained as evidence of an amount, not converted into invented issuance IDs.

Original and supplemental filings retain their actual IDs, predecessor, destination, receive time and availability time. Available evidence and messages are delivered chronologically. A derived multi-record report is delivered no earlier than its latest backing record. Proposed filing material cites selected attachments without asserting that a new submission occurred. Bounded incident/probe reports remain incomplete. A diagnostic month or USD workspace context used to open a missing-input case never becomes measurement or billing evidence.

The general-denial fallback replays the same frozen message bytes, with matching text hashes, against the supported control read from `940cd45:tests/test_v4_routes_integration.py`. Its exact account/resource grant and original supported agreement stay separate from the heldout agreement. All four selected message occurrences reach that fallback inbox; their unresolved classifications are not counted as successful dispute reasoning.

## Remaining evidence and interface disagreements

- Individual incidents and probes do not attest a complete service month. They can establish an observed-interval floor, not the exact monthly amount expected by some gold fields.
- The frozen provider records contain cause information, while the current resource join requires cause attribution in the customer-source report. The runner records this source-role mismatch rather than signing a provider statement as if it were a customer attestation.
- The new `request_evidence` API requires an exact request timestamp. An interval-level probe does not supply one. The runner supports an explicitly supplied timestamp and otherwise reports the gap.
- Complete maintenance and prior-credit histories are absent. Known aggregate credit amounts do not provide issuance IDs or attest that the history is complete. No empty complete statement or zero credit history is fabricated.
- A later attachment can add evidence to a previously populated filing field. The current historical-field join treats changed reference lists as a conflict. The driver preserves the supplemental evidence and reports `SUPPLEMENT_FIELD_AUGMENTATION_UNSUPPORTED`.
- Some messages have no frozen filing receipt. The normal route refuses them; the independent general control cannot manufacture a receipt for the heldout case.
- The application does not produce every frozen requested decision or evidence request. Those are recorded as disagreements, separately from missing input evidence and the remaining narrative predicates.

Gold decoding happens only after cases, public manifests and independent controls have been sealed. The comparison layer projects supported credit before filing gates separately from remaining credit, exact fractions separately from decimal display, scoped fee bases and prior amounts separately from awards, and contractual cutoffs separately from observed receipt clocks. The default suite remains the original freeze. The additive suite explicitly inherits its declared expected base/period only during this post-observation phase.

## Reproduction

Run a fresh interpreter. The runner and its adjacent helper modules can be outside the product checkout selected by `--source`.

```sh
UPTIME_PUBLIC_EXTRACTIONS=/Users/ronoktanvir/.local/share/uptime-engineering/review/extractions \
UPTIME_MODEL_PROPOSITIONS=1 \
/Users/ronoktanvir/.local/share/uptime-engineering/venv/bin/python \
  scripts/blind_acceptance.py \
  --source /path/to/stable/product-checkout \
  --fixture-root /Users/ronoktanvir/.local/share/uptime-engineering/holdouts/fresh-initial \
  --receipt /private/operator-directory/new-original-receipt.json \
  --allow-input-projection
```

The default transport is a real numeric-loopback socket. Use `--transport testclient` only for in-process verification; its receipt always reports `network_proof: false`. There is no automatic transport downgrade. For the separately pinned additive suite, pass `--suite boundaries` and a fixture root containing the original files plus the `c33642b` boundary files. An unrelated external fixture requires its own explicit `--manifest-sha256`.

Input projection decodes only case selectors, requested account/resource selectors and declared input defaults. Expected values are skipped by the token scanner. An external `--input-plan` is also supported and must bind the exact selected manifest. Each execution needs a new receipt path. Exit 0 requires both gates; incomplete or disagreeing acceptance exits 1; receipt creation failure exits 2.
