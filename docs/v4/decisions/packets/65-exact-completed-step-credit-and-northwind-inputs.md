## Question and evidence

The existing `monthly-availability/1` interpreter parses complete source clauses, binds the agreement/product/plan/fee line, checks a complete UTC month and an as-received filing timestamp, then compares exact integer ratios against explicit tiers. Its `rate_for` and `generated_checks` are shared by ordinary onboarding and active evaluation. No completed-step formula exists. Expanding a step formula to finite approximate thresholds would add rounding and equality errors. Current negotiated pricing already resolves signed/effective amendments by service period, rejects cross-amendment unsplit allocations, excludes taxes/non-covered products and performs final line HALF_UP cents.

Root's financial contract is explicit: USD 12,000 monthly service fee; 99.9% uptime target; 10% credit for each COMPLETED 0.1 percentage-point shortfall; 100% cap; no intermediate rounding; final HALF_UP cents. An amendment signed February 20, effective March 1, 2026, changes the monthly price ceiling to USD 11,000. January has 135 eligible downtime minutes out of 44,640; February has 5 out of 40,320; March lacks complete usage coverage. February's invoice is issued March 1. The claim window is the full 30 calendar days following month end: exclusive cutoff = first instant of following month + 30 days.

Proposed exact source clause: “For Cloud Hosting on the Business plan, the monthly uptime target is 99.9%; each completed 0.1 percentage point of shortfall below the target earns a 10% service credit, capped at 100%.” A separate explicit clause states: “Round the resulting service credit once to the nearest cent, with half cents rounded up; do not round availability or shortfall before counting completed increments.” Existing measurement, affected-monthly-fee/future-invoice-remedy and filing clauses retain their supported meanings. Unconsumed context, alternatives such as any fraction/started steps, conflicting tiers, multiple step rules, zero step, missing cap or missing rounding remain unsupported. These are bounded affirmative templates, not heuristic legal prose inference.

The authored order form and amendment separately state the fee, service period, one complete monthly subscription unit, separate excluded tax, excluded premium support and one-time charges, signature specimen timestamps and source clauses. They become existing typed `pricing.Agreement`/`ContractVersion`/`Invoice` inputs with exact source citations. The fixture loader derives SLA `monthly_fee_cents` from the governing service line's `PricingResult.lines[].expected`, never from scenario answers or the overbilled invoice total. Incomplete/ambiguous price results cannot produce a fee binding. Any later correspondence join must preserve that same governing basis; January's invoiced and governing fee coincide, but March's do not.

## Options and recommendation

**A, recommended:** add one reusable exact operator in `uptime/credit_steps.py`, strict clause recognition in `vendor_terms.py`, and normal `vendor_eval.rate_for/generated_checks/evaluate` consumption. Keep the existing family but make `credit_steps` mutually exclusive with `tiers`; `tiers` remains an empty list for that operator. Proposed typed fields: product, plan, target_units, step_units, credit_units, cap_units, citation; percentage units retain the existing 10^6 units per percentage point. Add explicit `credit_rounding=nearest_cent_half_up` with its own source citation. Include new arithmetic dependency in semantic-version hashing. The workflow owner must include the operator and rounding in the existing active economic-terms hash, preserving monthly obligation identity/reservations.

Let T be total measured units, A available units, U=100×10^6, target and step in existing percent units. Compute `shortfall_numerator=max(0,target*T-U*A)` and `completed_steps=shortfall_numerator//(step*T)`. Credit units = min(completed_steps×credit_units, cap_units). This is an exact rational comparison/floor, independent of Decimal precision and with no float or intermediate availability quantization. Currency rounds only once after fee_cents×credit_units/U, HALF_UP to whole cents. Invalid/nonintegral measurement units, T≤0, A outside [0,T], unsupported percentage precision or out-of-range rule values refuse. Display percentages may be formatted separately but never feed arithmetic.

**B:** expand the formula into tiers. Reject because approximate boundaries and equality cannot substitute for the exact declared step operator; large/unbounded schedules also obscure the citation.

**C:** leave the production operator unchanged and hardcode three displayed outputs. Reject because this would bypass normal onboarding and evaluation. The standalone fixture loader may assemble typed inputs, but cannot implement another financial engine or add vendor-name branches.

Strongest objection to A: it broadens a shared financial schema and creates an additional source-template family; a clean demonstration can hide that limitation. Containment: strict full-clause matching, no vendor-name conditions, independent second-name/parameter vectors, full existing tier regression, candidate semantic pinning, and explicit unsupported alternate wording. The package is not evidence of independent model generalization, live vendor entitlement, actual human approval or Ramp write/readback.

February 5/40,320 is complete and ineligible; its mathematical credit is zero but the ordinary noneligible case amount remains `null` per existing API conventions. One microsecond before cutoff is timely; cutoff is late. Missing received timestamp is unknown, not a timely filing or a late receipt.
* Verify final HALF_UP cent ties and independence from global Decimal precision. A generous fixture expected value cannot make an incorrect source operator pass independent boundary checks.
* Keep facts and expectations in separate files. Loader computation never imports expectations. Original source slices and hashes must match every citation. Signature records are verifiable LOCAL TEST specimens with no external certificate or assertion that real parties executed an agreement; no fixture private key grants operational reviewer authority.
* Negative clauses: “any started 0.1”, “per 0.1 rounded up”, missing cap, cap above100%, duplicate/conflicting step rule, simultaneous tier and step rule, extra contradictory condition, malformed/NaN/negative/oversized values and omitted rounding. Exact all-source checks refuse them.
