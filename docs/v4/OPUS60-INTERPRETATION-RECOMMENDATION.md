## Qualified or rejected advice

**Awards:** The adviser recommends a new two-tier award policy for every parser and bridge path. The concern is valid: semantic recognition does not verify issuance, posting or application. However, existing reported `awarded` money is already distinct from provider-confirmed attribution, posted and applied credit. Do not silently change historical financial semantics or claim that regex recognition gives stronger evidence than reviewed interpretation. A new bridge must initially keep an interpreted award informational and leave the ledger unchanged. Any later exact-authorized reported-award action must use the same source/identity/terms checks as the existing path; provider attribution and native application still require their current independent evidence. Whether to require a separate structured vendor artifact for *all* reported awards is a distinct financial-policy decision, not an incidental parser refactor.

**Two-person review:** The existing independent issuer/reviewer mandate structure already separates permissions, but that does not establish two actual human semantic reviews. Preserve that boundary. Do not claim a second human participated unless they did; a new threshold or policy for dual human review requires an explicit product decision.

**Blanket reapproval for public calls:** The actual consultation was explicitly requested and authorized. Reversible local schemas, UI and negative tests remain within engineering authorization. The proposed runtime adapter stays disabled by default, with no customer/private-evaluator processing, autonomous expenditure or vendor/financial action. A general demand to reconfirm every public synthetic call is not itself a new repository permission rule. Root should decide the exact bounded runtime test allowance and record usage; any private-data disclosure or real external financial effect retains its independent permission requirement. Do not represent tool-free local invocation as a provider retention guarantee.

**Evidence claims:** The studied cohort improvement remains historical context, not support for LLM superiority. Public fixtures and an adviser recommendation do not establish generalization. The independent corpus, any future public model extraction run, the manual-review benchmark and completed authorized workflows require separate results and denominators.
