Northbank Analytics | Demonstration customer agreement
Synthetic fixture. Version NB-DEMO-2025-10. Both parties and all transactions are fictional.

REBATE | Section R-1. Annual usage rebate.
For the contract year October 1, 2025 through September 30, 2026 inclusive,
count paid charges for Core and Analytics posted during that year. Subtract
returns posted in that year on those products. Exclude other products.
If net eligible spend is at least USD 100,000.00, a rebate of 2.00% applies
to the entire net eligible spend, rounded once to the nearest cent, half up.
Deduct all previously issued credits identified against R-1 for that contract
year. The agreement supplies no foreign-currency conversion rule.

NOTICE | Section N-2. Renewal notice receipt.
For the November 9, 2026 renewal, a notice must be received through the
designated customer portal by 17:00 America/Los_Angeles on the third working
day before the renewal date. Count backward, excluding the renewal date.
Calendar NB-OFFICE-2026 is authoritative. Saturday and Sunday are closed,
as is November 6, 2026, the vendor's scheduled office closure. A matching
portal receipt timestamp controls. A dispatch timestamp does not establish
receipt. This is a nonmonetary obligation.

PARITY | Section M-3. Negotiated price parity.
A same-period, same-product, same-volume commitment comparator furnished
with permission to use its terms is required for a parity determination.
This fixture includes no authorized comparator, pricing method or adjustment
amount. Any monetary determination requires a separately verified method.
