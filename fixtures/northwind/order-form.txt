Only recorded service unavailability counts as eligible downtime; no maintenance or other exclusions apply under this order. A complete monthly coverage report is required; absent coverage means eligibility is unknown.

Claims must be received during the 30 full calendar days following the final day of the service month, inclusive through the last instant of day 30. A draft timestamp is not a filing receipt.
