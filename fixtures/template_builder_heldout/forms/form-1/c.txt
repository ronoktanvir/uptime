SERVICE LEVEL ADDENDUM
to the Master Subscription Agreement dated as of September 22, 2026

This Service Level Addendum (this "Addendum") is entered into as of October 1, 2026 (the "Addendum Effective Date") by and between Larkspire Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Vendor"), and Tidewater Arcfield Holdings, Inc., a Maryland corporation ("Customer"). Vendor and Customer are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Vendor and Customer are parties to that certain Master Subscription Agreement dated as of September 22, 2026 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the "Agreement"), pursuant to which Vendor provides Customer with access to the Covered Service (as defined below) in exchange for the payment of fees;

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to set forth the level of availability that Vendor commits to maintain for the Covered Service, the manner in which such availability shall be measured, and the service credits that shall be available to Customer in the event that Vendor fails to meet such commitment; and

WHEREAS, this Addendum is intended to supplement, and to form an integral part of, the Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein and in the Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Addendum shall have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. As used in this Addendum, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

(a) "Availability" means the percentage calculated in accordance with Section 3.2.

(b) "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorised or required by law to close.

(c) "Covered Service" means the production instance of Vendor's hosted software-as-a-service offering known as Larkspire GridLens, as identified in the applicable Order Form, excluding any sandbox, test, staging, beta or trial environment.

(d) "Downtime" means any period of one (1) or more consecutive minutes during which the Covered Service is Unavailable, other than Excluded Downtime.

(e) "Emergency Maintenance" means maintenance that Vendor reasonably determines must be performed on an expedited basis to address a security vulnerability, an imminent threat to the integrity of the Covered Service or Customer Data, or a material defect affecting the Covered Service.

(f) "Measurement Period" means each calendar month during the Subscription Term; provided, however, that if the Subscription Term commences or ends on a day other than the first or last day of a calendar month, the resulting partial calendar month shall constitute a Measurement Period, and Availability for such partial month shall be calculated by reference only to the minutes falling within the Subscription Term.

(g) "Monitoring System" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

(h) "Monthly Fees" means the subscription fees paid or payable by Customer for the Covered Service in respect of the applicable Measurement Period, exclusive of taxes, professional services fees, overage charges and pass-through expenses, which, as of the Addendum Effective Date, are thirty-eight thousand seven hundred fifty United States dollars (US$38,750) per Measurement Period.

(i) "Scheduled Maintenance" means planned maintenance of the Covered Service of which Vendor has given notice in accordance with Section 5.1.

(j) "Service Credit" means a credit, calculated as a percentage of Monthly Fees, issued to Customer in accordance with Section 7.

(k) "Unavailable" means that the Covered Service fails to return a successful response to a request issued by the Monitoring System within thirty (30) seconds, as determined from not fewer than two (2) of the monitoring locations described in Section 3.1.

2. AVAILABILITY COMMITMENT

2.1 Vendor shall make the Covered Service available to Customer such that Availability during each Measurement Period is not less than ninety-nine and ninety-five hundredths percent (99.95%) (the "Availability Commitment").

2.2 The Availability Commitment shall apply solely to the Covered Service and shall not apply to any Third-Party Products, integrations or services not provided by Vendor, notwithstanding that the same may be accessed through or in connection with the Covered Service.

3. MEASUREMENT OF AVAILABILITY

3.1 Vendor shall monitor the Covered Service by means of automated probes issued at intervals of not more than one (1) minute from not fewer than three (3) geographically distinct monitoring locations (collectively, the "Monitoring System"), and the records generated by the Monitoring System shall be presumed accurate for purposes of this Addendum; provided, however, that Customer may rebut such presumption by furnishing reasonably detailed contrary evidence, including system logs or timestamped error records, in connection with any claim made under Section 7.4.

3.2 Availability for each Measurement Period shall be calculated in accordance with the following formula, with the result expressed as a percentage:

Availability = ((Total Minutes - Excluded Minutes - Downtime Minutes) / (Total Minutes - Excluded Minutes)) x 100

where "Total Minutes" means the total number of minutes in the Measurement Period, "Excluded Minutes" means the total number of minutes of Excluded Downtime in the Measurement Period, and "Downtime Minutes" means the total number of minutes of Downtime in the Measurement Period.

3.3 Availability shall be rounded to two (2) decimal places, with any value of five (5) or greater in the third decimal place rounded upward.

3.4 Vendor shall, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each Measurement Period, make available to Customer through Vendor's customer portal a report setting forth the Availability for such Measurement Period, together with a summary of each incident of Downtime and Excluded Downtime recorded during such Measurement Period.

4. EXCLUSIONS

4.1 The following shall constitute "Excluded Downtime" and shall not be counted as Downtime for any purpose under this Addendum:

(a) Scheduled Maintenance, up to an aggregate of eight (8) hours in any Measurement Period;

(b) Emergency Maintenance, up to an aggregate of two (2) hours in any Measurement Period;

(c) any unavailability caused by the acts or omissions of Customer, its Affiliates or its Users, including any use of the Covered Service in breach of the Agreement or the Documentation;

(d) any unavailability caused by failures of the public internet, Customer's networks or equipment, or any telecommunications or hosting provider not engaged by Vendor;

(e) any unavailability arising from a Force Majeure Event;

(f) any suspension of the Covered Service effected by Vendor in accordance with the Agreement; and

(g) any unavailability of features designated by Vendor as beta, preview or early access.

4.2 Any Scheduled Maintenance or Emergency Maintenance in excess of the applicable limit set forth in Section 4.1(a) or Section 4.1(b) shall be counted as Downtime.

5. SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE

5.1 Vendor shall give Customer not less than ninety-six (96) hours' advance notice of any Scheduled Maintenance, which notice shall be delivered by email to Customer's designated administrative contact and posted on Vendor's service status page and shall specify the anticipated start time, expected duration and, to the extent known, the anticipated effect on the Covered Service.

5.2 Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to conduct Scheduled Maintenance between 10:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Saturdays and Sundays.

5.3 Vendor shall notify Customer of any Emergency Maintenance as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event prior to its commencement where practicable.

6. REPORTING OF DOWNTIME

6.1 Customer may report suspected Downtime by submitting a ticket through Vendor's support portal or by email to support@larkspire.example, and each such report shall include a description of the issue, the time at which it was first observed and the Users affected.

6.2 Vendor shall acknowledge each such report within one (1) hour after receipt and shall update its service status page at intervals not exceeding sixty (60) minutes until the Downtime has been resolved.

6.3 For any incident of Downtime exceeding thirty (30) consecutive minutes, Vendor shall deliver to Customer, within five (5) Business Days after resolution, a written root cause analysis describing the cause of the incident and the remedial measures taken or planned.

7. SERVICE CREDITS

7.1 If Availability in any Measurement Period is less than the Availability Commitment, Vendor shall issue to Customer a Service Credit in the amount set forth below:

Availability in Measurement Period                Service Credit (% of Monthly Fees)
----------------------------------------------    ----------------------------------
Less than 99.95% but equal to or greater than 99.5%           15%
Less than 99.5%                                                35%

The threshold of ninety-nine and one-half percent (99.5%) set forth above is referred to herein as the "Lower Threshold."

7.2 Only one (1) Service Credit tier shall apply to any Measurement Period, being the tier corresponding to the Availability actually achieved, and Service Credits under the two (2) tiers shall not be cumulative.

7.3 The aggregate amount of all Service Credits issued in respect of any Measurement Period shall not exceed fifty percent (50%) of the Monthly Fees for such Measurement Period.

7.4 To receive a Service Credit, Customer shall submit a written claim to sla-claims@larkspire.example no later than forty-five (45) days after the last day of the Measurement Period to which the claim relates, which claim shall identify the dates and times of the Downtime alleged; provided, however, that Customer's failure to submit a claim within such period shall constitute a waiver of any Service Credit for such Measurement Period.

7.5 Vendor shall review each claim and notify Customer of its determination within ten (10) Business Days after receipt, and any dispute regarding such determination shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the Agreement.

7.6 Each Service Credit shall be applied as a credit against the next invoice for Monthly Fees issued by Vendor following Vendor's approval of the claim; provided, however, that if the Agreement expires or is terminated and no further invoice is to be issued, Vendor shall pay the amount of any approved Service Credit to Customer by refund within thirty (30) days, unless the Agreement was terminated by Vendor for Customer's uncured material breach. Except as provided in this Section 7.6, Service Credits shall have no cash value.

7.7 Customer shall not be entitled to any Service Credit for any Measurement Period during which any undisputed fees owed by Customer under the Agreement were more than thirty (30) days past due.

8. CHRONIC FAILURE

8.1 If Availability is less than the Lower Threshold in three (3) consecutive Measurement Periods, or in any four (4) Measurement Periods within any rolling twelve (12) month period, Customer may, by written notice given within sixty (60) days after the end of the last such Measurement Period, terminate the Agreement with effect thirty (30) days after the date of such notice, whereupon Vendor shall refund to Customer any prepaid fees attributable to the period following the effective date of termination.

9. SOLE REMEDY; RELATIONSHIP TO AGREEMENT

9.1 The Service Credits set forth in Section 7 and the termination right set forth in Section 8 shall constitute Customer's sole and exclusive remedies, and Vendor's sole and exclusive liability, for any failure by Vendor to meet the Availability Commitment.

9.2 This Addendum is incorporated into and forms part of the Agreement, and in the event of any conflict between this Addendum and the Agreement with respect to the subject matter hereof, this Addendum shall prevail; provided, however, that nothing in this Addendum shall expand any limitation of liability set forth in the Agreement.

9.3 This Addendum shall remain in effect for so long as the Agreement remains in effect and shall terminate automatically upon the expiry or termination of the Agreement, save that Sections 7.6 and 8.1 shall survive to the extent necessary to give effect to any accrued Service Credit or refund.

9.4 This Addendum may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and signatures delivered electronically shall be deemed original signatures.
